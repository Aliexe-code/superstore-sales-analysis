# Superstore Sales Analysis

A 4-day retail analytics project that answers business questions about revenue, profit, and customer behavior from the public Superstore dataset, producing a reproducible notebook and stakeholder-facing findings.

## Language

### Analysis

**Analysis Question**:
A business question answerable from the Superstore data, paired with why it matters and the chart type that will answer it.
_Avoid_: vague question, generic EDA prompt

**Finding**:
A specific claim tied to a number computed in the notebook and its supporting chart (e.g., "Technology generated $836K, 36% of total sales").
_Avoid_: observation, insight, takeaway without a number

**Interpretation**:
Two to three plain-English sentences beneath a chart explaining what the numbers mean for the business.
_Avoid_: code description, chart description without meaning

**Limitation**:
An honest statement of what the analysis cannot tell you due to data gaps or scope.
_Avoid_: excuse, future work without constraint

### Data

**Raw Data**:
The Kaggle CSV saved untouched as `data/superstore.csv` (9,994 rows, 21 columns, 2014-01-03 to 2017-12-30).
_Avoid_: original data, source file

**Cleaned Data**:
The dataset after documented cleaning steps, saved as `data/superstore_clean.csv` or regenerated by the notebook.
_Avoid_: processed data, final data

**Visualization**:
A chart with title, axis labels, and legend that answers one Analysis Question; at least three chart types are required.
_Avoid_: plot, graph, figure without interpretation

### Delivery

**Main Notebook**:
`Superstore_Analysis.ipynb` at the repo root — the single reproducible deliverable that runs top-to-bottom on a fresh clone.
_Avoid_: supporting notebook, final notebook in subfolder

**Supporting Notebook**:
A notebook in `notebooks/` (01_questions_and_setup, 02_cleaning, 03_eda_exploration) used to develop work that is later consolidated into the Main Notebook.
_Avoid_: draft notebook, exploratory notebook as deliverable

**Checkpoint**:
A mentor review at end of Day 16 (mid-project, progress check) and end of Day 18 (final pass/fail against the 36-item checklist).
_Avoid_: meeting, review without criteria

**Walkthrough**:
A 2-minute verbal presentation (target 1:45–2:15) where the intern states findings with numbers and one Limitation without reading the notebook.
_Avoid_: presentation, demo, slideshow
